### 🚀 Featured Projects

#### 📉 [Customer Churn Prediction](https://github.com/shreyaslande/telco-customer-churn-prediction)
End-to-end ML project on **7,032 telecom customer records**: cleaning, EDA, and feature engineering (tenure groups, add-on service count, average monthly charge), then a comparison of Logistic Regression, Random Forest and Gradient Boosting.
**Best model: Gradient Boosting — ROC-AUC 0.84, 79.7% accuracy.** Key finding: month-to-month customers churn at **43%** vs **3%** on two-year contracts; tenure, fibre-optic service and electronic-check payment are the strongest churn signals.

#### 🚢 [Titanic Survival Analysis](https://github.com/shreyaslande/titanic-survival-analysis)
Exploratory data analysis of **891 Titanic passenger records** with Python, Pandas and Matplotlib. Handled missing data (median imputation for Age, dropped the 77%-empty Cabin column) and presented findings through 5 charts — survival was strongly linked to gender (**74%** of women vs **19%** of men survived), ticket class (**63%** in 1st class vs **24%** in 3rd) and fare paid.
